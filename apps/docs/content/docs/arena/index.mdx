title: Arena
description: Gamified, sport-themed components built on SVG courts and the Matos UI surface and motion tokens.

Arena is a family of playful, sport-themed components. Each one draws its court
or field as an SVG and animates the action on it — a rally crossing the net, a
ball arcing to the hoop, a formation shifting shape. They are meant to feel like
a game, not a dashboard widget.

They still obey the rest of the system. Surfaces use `Elevated` and `offset`,
never a fixed background. Timing comes from the
[Motion tokens](/docs/foundations/motion), and the high-bounce `playful` tier is
opt-in through a prop — off by default, so a component reads as sober until you
ask it to celebrate. Reduced motion keeps the state changes and drops the
travel, and `arena-motion` (installed alongside every Arena component) keeps
that preference live during a session.

## Rules of the section

- **Accent colour follows the palette.** No pitch green, no clay orange. If a
  component needs a semantic colour of its own it exposes a local custom
  property with a token-derived default, overridable at the call site.
- **No real IP.** No club crests, league names, competitions or athletes — in
  components, demos, or example copy. Sample data is invented and generic.
- **No illustrations, mascots or emoji.** The section is typographic and
  geometric. A Lucide line icon is the ceiling.
- **The component contract applies in full.** Forwarded `ref`, merged
  `className`, `data-slot`, text passed in as props.

## v1

- [Rally Court](/docs/arena/rally-court) — a tennis scoreboard on an SVG court:
  full point → game → set scoring with deuce and advantage, an animated rally,
  and a serve side that flips.

More sports (a basketball hoop shot, a soccer formation board, a stadium hype
meter) land in the next round once the section has proven itself.
